How to play

Desktop: WASD/arrows move, Shift runs, Space jumps, Q dodges, E acts. Hold E near people to help or at the barricade to break through. Drag to turn the camera. P/Escape pauses; J opens the City map.

Phone: left joystick moves; push toward its outer edge to run. Right thumb drags the world to look. Tap Dodge or Jump, and hold the nearby Action to help or open a gate. Follow the current objective: protect the recorder, escort both companions, or support and sustain the gathering. City map selects districts and opens optional accounts. Story assist slows police; the separate slower story offers dialogue choices and a rhythm skip.

Rendered live in 3D. A source-referenced, compressed setting, not a surveyed map or reconstruction of actual incidents. Characters and story are fictional. Still a development build, not a finished photorealistic release.
