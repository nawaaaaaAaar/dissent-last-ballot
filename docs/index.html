Movement & story controls

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk. Shift to run, Space to jump, E to interact. Drag the scene to turn the camera; movement follows its direction. P/Escape pauses.

Phone: use the left joystick to move and drag the world to turn the camera. Run, jump and nearby interaction buttons are on screen. Complete the five connected story events; joining the protest is optional.

Rendered live in 3D. A source-referenced, compressed setting, not a surveyed map or reconstruction of actual incidents. Characters and story are fictional. Still a development build, not a finished photorealistic release.
