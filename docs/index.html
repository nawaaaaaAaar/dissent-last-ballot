Build the network.

Phone: left stick moves and drives; its outer edge runs on foot. Hold the right stick to aim and repeatedly strike, or hold Strike for nearby aim assist. Dodge gives a short escape burst. In the van, release the left stick to slow down, or hold Brake. Corner assist slows sharp turns.

Desktop: WASD/arrows move or steer. Shift runs; Space aims at the nearest visible opponent or blocking cordon and chains strikes (or brakes in a van). Q dodges, E acts; hold E to copy a dispatch or restore aid. Z switches close/overview camera, P pauses, M opens missions. Mouse aiming is optional. Teal marks Aman; gold marks Kabir. Guards carry shields: flank them or finish three strikes. Expanding red rings warn of attacks. Damage lands at the arm's contact phase; dodging or moving out of reach can make a strike miss. Strikes and dodges spend stamina: disengage to recover.

  1. Start near the Jantar street gathering. Your volunteer van is a short walk away; Action/E boards it, even before the mission objective is complete. Follow the gold radar route toward the operation. M or Map & Missions opens the full map.
  2. Rescue: approach the fictional witness cordon, evade or interrupt the guards, then Action/E near Kabir. Three contact hits open the yellow line; a low bench side route can bypass it. A tall banner screen blocks sight: face it and tap Action/E to move it, drawing attention to that sound. Face a low bench or table and tap Action/E to vault it when the landing is clear. Bring Kabir back to the van.
  3. Courier: exit at Tolstoy Marg or India Gate and hold Action/E at the gold dispatch for 1.8 seconds. Damage interrupts copying; progress is retained. Protect it and reach the green destination. India Gate is a fictional relay, not a claim of a current protest there.
  4. Rally: use the gathering screen for cover and secure the first point for eight cumulative seconds with guards outside the circle. At the aid table, clear space and hold Action/E for 2.8 seconds. At the reading area, bring both readers to the Jantar assembly on foot. They may brace when attacks pass nearby, then follow again; arriving alone does not finish the operation.
  5. For deliveries, lose sight of pursuers until heat is below one bar, then slow down at the destination. Gold medals reward keeping health and avoiding vehicle crashes, not a destruction tally.
  6. After a win, choose another job. Finish the three different operations to unlock the charter run. Repeating jobs changes guard approaches. Support credits buy strike tempo, van protection or running endurance. The next network cycle continues after the charter.

Medkits restore health. Volunteer support restores stamina, health and van condition once per job after you break pursuit. Retry restores arrival, rescue, mission-stage or boarding checkpoints within this tab. Some repeats add a roadblock: bypass it or use an abstract arcade strike. There are no real personal records or mandatory case quizzes.
