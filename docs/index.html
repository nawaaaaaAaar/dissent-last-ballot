Controls & mission

Phone: Swipe left/right to change lanes, up to jump, down to slide. Or use the large on-screen buttons. Tap the mission button during story moments.

Keyboard: Arrow keys or A/D to move, Space/W to jump, S to slide. E to interact, P/Escape to pause.

Collect at least 3 evidence packets, preserve the journalist's footage, and reach the archive. Assist mode reduces collision damage. Nothing is saved when you close the page.

A playable vertical slice with original procedural 3D art. Not a finished or photorealistic release. Fictional scenes, not a reconstruction or real-world sabotage guide.
