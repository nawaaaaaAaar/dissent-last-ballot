How to play

Desktop: WASD/arrows move, Shift runs, Space jumps, Q dodges, E acts. Hold E near people to help or at the barricade to break through. Drag to turn the camera. P/Escape pauses; J opens the journal.

Phone: left joystick moves; drag the world to look. Run uses stamina. Tap Dodge to evade, and hold nearby Action buttons to help or break through. Recover the recorder, get Kabir moving and reach the assembly. Story assist slows police. The separate story mode offers dialogue choices and a rhythm skip.

Rendered live in 3D. A source-referenced, compressed setting, not a surveyed map or reconstruction of actual incidents. Characters and story are fictional. Still a development build, not a finished photorealistic release.
