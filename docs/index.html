Play before reading

Five verbs.
One way home.

Phone: left stick moves and drives; its outer edge runs on foot. Hold the right stick to aim and repeatedly strike, or hold Strike for nearby aim assist. Dodge gives a short escape burst. In the van, release the left stick to slow down, or hold Brake. Corner assist slows sharp turns.

Desktop: WASD/arrows move or steer the van. Shift runs, held Space repeatedly strikes (or brakes while driving), Q dodges, E rescues or enters/exits the van. Point with the mouse to aim. P/Escape pauses. Your teal ground ring identifies Aman; Kabir has a gold ring. Nearby opponents have three condition segments, and red circles show incoming attacks.

  1. Move up the central street toward the gold objective. Strike nearby fictional opponents or dodge their red wind-up circles. The yellow line can be broken with four strikes, or bypassed through a side street.
  2. Approach Kabir and clear or draw away his nearby guards, then press Action/E. Walk over the recording beside him.
  3. Reach the volunteer van at the central crossing. Wait for Kabir, then press Action/E to board together.
  4. Drive around the blocks. The west road is shorter but gains a roadblock; the east route is longer. Buildings block enemy sight. When SEARCH appears, keep out of sight until heat falls below one bar.
  5. Reach the green safe-house circle in the west, slow down and park. The mission only ends if Kabir and the recording are aboard and pursuit is lost.

Medkits restore health. On-foot health and vehicle condition can fail independently. Retry restores the latest rescue/boarding checkpoint within this tab. There are no mandatory case quizzes and no real personal records.
