Phone: left stick moves and drives; its outer edge runs on foot. Hold the right stick to aim and repeatedly strike, or hold Strike for nearby aim assist. Dodge gives a short escape burst. In the van, release the left stick to slow down, or hold Brake. Corner assist slows sharp turns.

Desktop: WASD/arrows move or steer the van. Shift runs, held Space repeatedly strikes (or brakes while driving), Q dodges, E rescues or enters/exits the van. Point with the mouse to aim. P/Escape pauses. Your teal ground ring identifies Aman; Kabir has a gold ring. Nearby opponents have three condition segments, and red circles show incoming attacks.

  1. Start at CP. Your volunteer van is beside you. Action/E boards it, even before the mission objective is complete. Follow the gold radar route toward the operation. M or Map & Missions opens the full map.
  2. Rescue: exit near Jantar Mantar, evade or interrupt the guards, then Action/E near Kabir. Four strikes open the yellow line; bypassing it is also valid. Bring Kabir back to the van.
  3. Courier: exit and walk over the gold dispatch at Tolstoy Marg or India Gate. Protect it and drive to the green destination. India Gate is a fictional network relay, not a claim of a current protest there.
  4. Rally: arrive on foot and secure three different gathering points: regroup, aid, then the reading circle. Each takes eight cumulative seconds in its green zone. When the marker moves, you must reposition. Dodge red warning circles, strike to interrupt, and return to the zone. Leaving pauses progress rather than erasing it.
  5. For deliveries, lose sight of pursuers until heat is below one bar, then slow down at the destination. Gold medals reward keeping health and avoiding vehicle crashes, not a destruction tally.
  6. After a win, choose another job. Finish the three different operations to unlock the charter run. Repeating jobs changes guard approaches. Support credits buy strike tempo, van protection or running endurance. The next network cycle continues after the charter.

Medkits restore health. On-foot health and vehicle condition can fail independently. Retry restores the latest rescue/boarding checkpoint within this tab. Some repeat runs put a red roadblock along the suggested route: bypass it, or exit and strike it open. There are no mandatory case quizzes and no real personal records.
