How to play

Desktop: WASD/arrows move, Shift runs, Space jumps hurdles, Q dodges, F activates Rally, E acts. Hold E to help or open the line. Drag to turn the camera. P/Escape pauses; J opens the optional journal.

Phone: left joystick moves and its outer edge runs. Right thumb turns the camera. Tap Jump, Dodge or Rally; hold nearby Action to help or open the route. Walk over three packet crates to collect them automatically. Supplies recharge stamina and Rally energy. Rally costs 30 energy with a six-second cooldown and briefly creates space from nearby pursuers. Keep moving in the gold gathering zone during the final hold. Case-reading and the charter are optional journal material, not required quizzes.

The movement demands Gyanesh Kumar’s exit and an end to SIR. Electoral-manipulation allegations are disputed. All voter files and camp locations are invented; gameplay does not register voters, abolish SIR or reverse elections. Live 3D development build, not photorealistic production art.
