# DISSENT: how to play and win

The current default is **At the Line**, one connected rescue-and-escape encounter. The older city campaign has its own guide in HOW-TO-WIN-v17.md; its map board, upgrades, right aiming stick and search-cooling conditions do not apply here.

## Controls

On a phone, move with the left joystick. Push it to its outer edge to run. Hold Strike for short combos; Dodge evades and can open a counterattack during a late red warning. Nearby visible opponents are faced automatically. The contextual button moves the screen, vaults the low side rail, frees Kabir or boards the van. Hold it when it says FREE KABIR.

Desktop uses WASD/arrows to move, Shift to run, Space to strike, Q to dodge and E to interact. Hold E for the rescue. P/Escape pauses. In the van, movement controls steering and acceleration; Q or Dodge/Brake slows the vehicle.

Attacks preserve dodge energy. Release Strike to recover and reposition. The shield resists frontal early strikes; the third push or an opened counter changes that. You do not have to defeat every opponent.

## Reach and rescue

Move north along the street toward the dark police transport. The first pair introduces the red committed-attack warning. The aid table on the left offers one recovery if you are hurt. Walk around its visible tabletop rather than pushing through it.

Kabir is on the left beside the bus. A screen can block direct sight; move it with the contextual action or approach around its left. Create room, get close to Kabir and hold FREE KABIR. His line confirms the recording is with him.

## Cross together

The middle route needs three close barricade contacts. Face the yellow line and stay within arm reach; opening it changes Kabir's path. The low left rail offers an alternative: get close until VAULT THE SIDE RAIL appears, then use the action. Kabir approaches and crosses after you.

Kabir briefly braces when attacks threaten him, then follows again. Do not assume that sprinting far ahead means he is already at the van. Make space and keep an eye on him. Opponents can be left behind.

## Drive to the shelter

The cream volunteer van is beyond the line. When GET IN TOGETHER appears, board; WAIT FOR KABIR means he is not close enough yet.

Angle into the left lane, then straighten. The orange roadworks occupy the right side; pushing into them repeatedly damages the van. A right-lane patrol approaches under a warning. Give it lateral space rather than staying on its line.

Reach the community shelter beyond the works. Winning requires the van, Kabir and the recording, not a score threshold. Vehicle failure offers Retry the escape, retaining the rescue; on-foot capture restarts the encounter. Replay starts a fresh scene, without earned upgrades.
