# DISSENT: every eligible voter

## Current implementation: v0.10

Signal Run retains the researched movement and broader reform demands below, but removes compulsory case panels from the action route. Players carry three fictional record packets per chapter, help people directly, regroup companions and hold the gathering. Cases remain optional in the journal; the ending states the three commitments without a mandatory charter gate. The v0.9 implementation descriptions below are design history, not the current control flow. See [the action design](https://github.com/nawaaaaaAaar/dissent-last-ballot/blob/main/GAME-FEEL.md).

This v0.9 revision follows the creator's request to focus on the current electoral-accountability movement, not the earlier Jamia/Shaheen Bagh protest histories. Its playable thesis is “replacement is not repair”: leadership accountability, ending the contested SIR process, and inclusion with transparent review must remain connected. These are movement demands inside a fictional game, not announcements that they have been achieved.

## Terms and current evidence

“Vote-verifier,” “sir” and “sar” are provisionally interpreted as the vote-chori movement and **SIR, Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls**. The official's name is **Gyanesh Kumar**, Chief Election Commissioner; AP's October 2 report describes demands for his resignation and suspension of SIR, and the Commission/government's denial of wrongdoing ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/india-sir-cjp-election-commission-gyanesh-protests-1d43de28dfd6fa920133d98e9d0e8e35)).

October 4 reporting describes protests in Delhi and Mumbai demanding Kumar's departure, including calls to scrap the roll revisions, and says the Commission describes its purpose as removing duplicate, deceased or ineligible entries while maintaining electoral integrity ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/protests-demanding-indian-election-chief-resigns-escalate-2026-10-04/)). Claims that eligible voters were removed for partisan advantage remain allegations disputed by the Commission and BJP, not a finding made by this game ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/protests-demanding-indian-election-chief-resigns-escalate-2026-10-04/)).

The dated primary documents establish a concrete inclusion issue, not just a slogan. The Commission's September 26 note says SIR had been completed in 20 states/UTs, specifically including Bihar and West Bengal, and directs a drive for left-out and young/first-time electors ([ECI press note](https://www.eci.gov.in/eci/public/api/document?id=17536)). Its October 1 letter directs BLOs to compare pre-SIR and current rolls, identify earlier-listed people missing now, conduct house-to-house status checks, and facilitate enrolment through Form 6 wherever the person is eligible; it also directs assistance for first-time/young electors and camps for marginalised people ([ECI letter](https://www.eci.gov.in/eci/public/api/document?id=17541)).

A separate official statement, posted September 23, says major elections in Bihar, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Assam and West Bengal had recently been conducted; it also says decisions including SIR were unanimous ([PIB/ECI statement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2313884&reg=48&lang=1)). The publication dates come from the documents' own text; some search metadata describes later page updates. Completed SIR and completed elections are different facts, so neither is inferred from the other.

Reporting raises a further unresolved problem: people with pending Bengal appeals may not benefit in the same way from the new drive, and political leaders have asked for alternatives ([The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/rights/bengal-sir-appellants-may-not-benefit-from-special-drive-concerns/amp)). That is a reported concern, not a proven blanket rule or an adopted remedy. The game's appeal file therefore stays “referred / pending,” never “restored” merely because the player clicks a button.

## What changes in the game

The three existing authored environments are reused, but their purposes and mechanics change. The second and third spaces are openly labelled invented voter-help camps linked to Bihar and West Bengal; they are not actual camp addresses, protest sites or geographic reconstructions. The historical Jamia/Shaheen Bagh narrative is no longer the current campaign.

- **Delhi / More than one resignation:** Recover the recorder, review three fictional consented voter files at help points, cross the authored line and reunite with Kabir. The demands name Gyanesh Kumar and SIR, but the police encounter is fictional, not an allegation that a named person ordered a specific assault.
- **Bihar / Names are people:** Compare an eligible omitted resident, a first-time eligible adult, and a duplicate with one valid entry remaining. Regroup the referral team and deliver together. Both companions must arrive; case referrals are required, not optional flavour.
- **West Bengal / No case disappears:** Review a pending appeal, an eligible omission and a first-time voter file; support all three desks and sustain them under abstract pressure. Then finish the reform charter.

Each invented file displays a pre-SIR record, current record and resident's account. The three responses are inclusion assistance, recognition that one valid entry remains, or pending-appeal support. A wrong answer gives feedback and another attempt; it does not punish a real person or erase a record. Leaving the panel produces no referral. Action pauses during reading, so comprehension is not a reflex-speed test.

The final assembly offers a resignation-only resolution. That is deliberately insufficient: the player must select leadership accountability, ending the contested SIR process, and inclusion/appeal support across affected states. The fictional win is adoption of this complete demand, not a simulated legal judgment, automatically successful voter registration, policy repeal or reversal of elections already held.

## Design rationale and boundaries

Exploration and action still supply movement decisions, stamina, dodge, pursuit, escort responsibility and retries. The new comparison decisions make the movement's theme playable rather than simply putting new slogans on old props. Each chapter teaches a different distinction: missing does not always mean wrongly deleted; eligible new voters need support too; an unresolved appeal must not disappear into a false completion label.

The game supports the creator's resistance framing without requiring unverified criminal claims, official endorsement, real-person likenesses or personal voter records. All characters and files are invented adults. Names of public officials and movement demands are contextual; no attack on an official is a mission. The barricade encounter remains abstract authored fiction, not a sabotage tutorial.

“Every voter” is implemented as **every eligible voter**, with transparent review of omissions and disputed cases, not retention of deceased or duplicate entries. The game does not recommend how to complete actual declarations, determine citizenship, submit a real appeal, or contact a campaign. Real procedures can change and must be checked with the relevant electoral authority.

There is no real voter-data upload, party-targeting system, petition submission or external political messaging. Progress stays local where browser storage is permitted. The v0.9 campaign uses a separate save key from v0.8 so old completions cannot count as new case reviews.

## Verification and remaining limits

Acceptance requires all nine referrals through real controls, wrong-answer feedback, cancel without progress, paused review, group arrival, sustain completion, rejection of an incomplete charter, full charter adoption, mobile held interaction and touch answers, map/replay/resume, and portrait/landscape fit. The supplied game client also checks movement and jumping. Public verification and actual rendering captures are recorded in [QA](https://github.com/nawaaaaaAaar/dissent-last-ballot/blob/main/QA.md).

This is a theme-and-mechanics revision using existing geometry and art. It is not new photogrammetry, authentic regional architecture, richer facial acting or a photorealistic production release. Physical-phone/Safari performance and first-time-player enjoyment remain separate unverified acceptance gates.
