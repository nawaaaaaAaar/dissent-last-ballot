# DISSENT: Signal Run

## The decision

“More game” means making movement, timing and recovery the main experience. v0.10 replaces the compulsory nine-case route with an action campaign: collect three record packets per district, avoid pressure, help people directly, and finish a distinct local objective. Electoral-case reading remains optional in the journal. The political story remains, but completing a questionnaire is no longer the main challenge.

## The playable loop

- **Move and collect:** Packets are picked up by proximity. Supplies restore sprint stamina and Rally energy. Consecutive packet pickups increase the score multiplier; taking damage breaks the chain.
- **Read and react:** Officers signal an approaching hit with a red ground ring and a brief wind-up. Jump clears low crates; Dodge gives a short forward dash and protection. Red area warnings still demand movement.
- **Create breathing room:** Rally uses 30 energy, has a six-second cooldown and briefly pushes back/stuns nearby fictional pursuers. It is an abstract arcade ability, not a real-world tactic or a reward for injuring people.
- **Commit under pressure:** Held Action helps people or opens a fictional barrier. Jantar requires a recorder, breakthrough and reunion; Bihar requires both companions to arrive; Bengal requires support for three stations and twenty game seconds inside the gathering ring.
- **Recover and replay:** Failure offers a checkpoint retry. Completed chapters and best runs persist locally when storage is available. Supplies, help bonuses, health, time and packet chains support another attempt.

## Why these changes

The game-feel literature describes perceived control as a combination of input response and readable audiovisual feedback, while stressing that more effects do not automatically mean a better experience ([Designing Game Feel: A Survey](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2011.09201)). This pass therefore adds a visible energy meter, cooldown, pickup feedback, directional pushback and restrained dash-camera response rather than covering the scene with effects.

Readable attack anticipation gives players an opportunity to choose a response rather than treating damage as an unexplained punishment ([Designing for Difficulty: Readability in ARPGs](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/designing-for-difficulty-readability-in-arpgs)). The officer wind-up and ground ring apply that principle to this prototype; neither source establishes that these particular mechanics will be enjoyable.

## Narrative and limits

The campaign still carries leadership accountability, ending the contested SIR process and transparent inclusion/appeal support for every eligible voter. The ending states those demands without requiring a charter checkbox gate. Research, allegations, official responses and invented encounters remain distinguished in [the movement brief](https://github.com/nawaaaaaAaar/dissent-last-ballot/blob/main/CURRENT-MOVEMENT.md).

This is an action-design iteration using the existing world and character art. It does not expand the map or solve realism, authored jump/dodge animation, facial performance or physical-phone performance. Automated completion is not a novice enjoyment study. The next meaningful evaluation is whether players understand the first objective, react to warnings and want another run, not whether another explanatory panel can be added.
